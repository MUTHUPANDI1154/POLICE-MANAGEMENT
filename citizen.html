Citizen Management

About Citizen Management

Citizen Management is an important part of the Tamil Nadu Police Management System.

It allows citizens to access police services, register complaints and view their complaint details.

Citizen Services

Service Description
Citizen Registration Citizens can create an account in the system.
Citizen Login Registered citizens can login to their account.
Complaint Registration Citizens can register complaints online.
Complaint Status Citizens can check the status of complaints.
FIR Information Citizens can view FIR related information.
